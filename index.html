How I Work

Calibrate. Map. Vet. Close.

A repeatable process that delivers <40 day time-to-fill without sacrificing quality.

01

Calibration

We go deep on your culture, tech stack, and what "great" really looks like. I don't start a search until I can describe the ideal candidate like I've worked with them.

02

Market Map

I map the top 100+ candidates in your niche — active and passive. I know where the hardware engineers are, which fintech companies are shedding talent, and who's doing cutting-edge ML work.

03

Vet & Curate

Multi-stage screening that checks technical depth, cultural alignment, and genuine readiness to move. You only see candidates who are a 90%+ match.

04

Close & Retain

Offer strategy, counter-offer coaching, resignation support, and onboarding check-ins. A hire isn't a success until they're thriving 12 months in.
